About

We are a non-profit organization in California run by students and educators who believe that every student should have the opportunity to discover a passion for engineering regardless of their situation.

Across Southern California, we connect students with mentors, tools, and teams that provide the skills and mentorship needed to reach their highest potential in STEM careers, regardless of background or experience.

Our Purpose

SoCal Robotics & STEM Foundation is organized for charitable and educational purposes: to provide youth mentorship and science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) education to students throughout Southern California, including robotics team programs and community workshops.
  • 🧑‍🏫 Youth mentorship
  • 🔧 Hands-on STEM
  • 🌎 Open to all students
  • 🤝 Community driven

FTC Team

Our high school team competes in the FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC), where students design, build, program, and drive a robot through a new game each season while learning engineering notebooks, project management, and outreach.

  • Mentor-led build seasons and practice
  • CAD, programming, and electronics skills
  • Competition travel and team leadership roles

SoCal Youth Mentorship

Beginner-friendly workshops and mentoring across Southern California that introduce younger students to robotics, coding, and creative problem-solving, guided by volunteers who have been there.

  • No experience needed
  • Build-and-take-home style challenges
  • Small-group mentoring and family-friendly demo days
